# Probability for Artificial Intelligence

Professors [Mehran Sahami](https://profiles.stanford.edu/mehran-sahami) and [Chris Piech](https://profiles.stanford.edu/christopher-piech) from Stanford University are offering [Probability for AI](https://pai.stanford.edu/) to the public free of charge. As an alumnus of their [Code in Place](https://codeinplace.stanford.edu/) course, I was delighted to receive an invitation to apply.

It feels like a wonderful way to kick off retirement - keeping my mind active and engaged alongside a stack of good books.

**First day of class**: October 9th 2026.

### Application process


The application process started with taking two lessons on [Probability](https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs109/cs109.1182/reader/2%20Probability.pdf) and [Conditional Probability](https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs109/cs109.1182/reader/3%20Conditional.pdf). 

As part of the application, I submitted the required assignment [Word Detective](https://pai.stanford.edu/pai/share/fpO8iOQvXD8cLu6QSbDg).
