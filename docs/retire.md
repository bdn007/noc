# It's time!

### Dear Colleagues,

After much thought and reflection, I would like to share with you that I have decided to retire by the end of this year.

I have spent many years away from home, and the past 20 years in Qatar have been a particularly meaningful chapter of my life. I am deeply grateful for the experiences, challenges, achievements, friendships, and memories that have made Qatar feel like home.

Now, I feel it is time to return home and spend more time with family, especially our aging mother and mother-in-law. Life reminds us that some moments and relationships cannot be postponed.

As I close this chapter, I sincerely thank you all for your friendship, support, and cooperation over the years. It has been a privilege to work alongside you, and I will always treasure the friendships and memories we have shared.

Retirement is a new beginning, but saying goodbye is never easy. I hope our paths will cross again and that our friendships will continue.

I wish you and your families happiness, good health, and success.

Thank you for being part of my story.
