# It's time

### Dear Colleagues,

After much thought and reflection, I would like to share with you that I have decided to retire by the end of this year.

I have spent many years away from home, building my career and embracing the opportunities and experiences that life has brought my way. The past 20 years in Qatar have been a particularly meaningful chapter of that journey. It has been a long and rewarding experience, filled with challenges, achievements, lasting friendships, and countless memories that I will always cherish. Qatar has been my home for two decades, and I am deeply grateful for all that this chapter of my life has given me.

I now feel that it is time for me to return home and devote more time to the people who matter most — our families, relatives, and friends. Most importantly, I want to be closer to and spend more precious time with our aging mother and mother-in-law. Life has reminded me that while work is an important part of our journey, there are moments and relationships that cannot be replaced or postponed.

As I prepare to close this chapter, I want to sincerely thank each of you for your friendship, support, cooperation, and the many wonderful memories we have shared over the years. It has been a privilege to work alongside such wonderful colleagues, and I am truly grateful for the relationships I have built during my career.

Although I am looking forward to retirement and returning home, saying goodbye to colleagues and friends is never easy. I hope that our paths will continue to cross, and that the friendships we have made will remain long after my working days come to an end.

Thank you once again for being part of my journey. I wish you and your families continued happiness, good health, and success.

### [A Few Words to Remember](/memwall)

As I begin this new chapter, I would be grateful if you could leave a few words, a message, or a favorite [memory](/memwall). Your thoughts and wishes will be a wonderful reminder of the people and friendships that have made this journey so special.

Thank you for being part of my story.

With warmest regards,

Bao Ngo
